# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose vulnerabilities, credentials or private data in a public
issue.

Use the contact link at [jord-andrade.dev](https://jord-andrade.dev/#contato)
and include:

- the affected URL or component;
- steps to reproduce the issue;
- the observed impact;
- a safe way to follow up.

You should receive an acknowledgement within five business days. This policy
applies to the portfolio source and its production deployment.
